Questions from decision makers

A few things you should know first.

Do you contact my client?

Not unless explicitly agreed. The default relationship is agency-to-contractor; your team manages the end customer, pricing and approvals.

Can you work entirely behind our brand?

Yes, for an agreed scope. Deliverables can use your agency's reporting template where supplied. The sample documents here deliberately identify the work as demonstrations.

What access is required for the QA pilot?

Usually just public or approved staging URLs and the list of pages to review. The starter QA pilot is read-only. Production changes are separately authorized and quoted.

Do you promise higher conversion rates or rankings?

No. I can examine usability and implementation risks, but actual performance improvement requires measurement, suitable traffic and often controlled testing.

What about WordPress, forms, analytics or CRM integrations?

Those require a separate platform-specific scope, approved access and verified implementation proficiency. They are not silently included in a QA or prototype quote.

How are agency materials and access handled?

Send only information needed to scope the task. The introductory QA pilot is read-only and normally requires no credentials. For additional work, access permissions, subcontracting, AI-tool use, confidentiality and retention are agreed in writing before files or client data are shared. Do not email passwords or sensitive client records.

Is the business dependent on AI-generated work?

No. AI may support drafting, organization and code review when allowed, but source verification, testing, confidentiality and deliverable acceptance remain human responsibilities.
