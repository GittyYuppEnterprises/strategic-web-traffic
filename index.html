Have a different question? Ask directly ↗
Can you work completely behind our agency's brand?

Yes, under the agreed scope. Your team manages the client. Deliverables can use a supplied agency template, and confidentiality/NDA terms can be arranged before materials are shared.

How fast is the five-page QA pilot?

Timing depends on the pages, access, current capacity and what's required to verify findings. I'll propose an actual delivery date before taking payment or starting the review—not advertise an unverified universal turnaround.

What happens if we disagree with a finding?

You can ask for clarification on the included report. I correct factual or reproducibility errors in my delivered findings without treating them as new paid scope. Additional pages, live fixes and additional testing are separate work.

How is AI used on agency work?

AI can assist with drafting and organization only when the specific engagement permits it. Sensitive materials are not placed in unapproved tools, and source checks and final acceptance remain my responsibility.

Can you help with WordPress, technical integrations or legal content?

Content preparation and implementation specifications can be scoped readily. Any CMS production access, form or analytics integration requires separate authorization and verified platform proficiency. Legal content requires appropriate attorney/client review.
