Privacy & contact information

This initial static website does not operate a form endpoint, web analytics, cookies or advertising pixels.

When you contact us

Clicking an email link opens your own email application. If you choose to send a message, it is handled through the email provider. The information you choose to share will be used to respond to your inquiry, evaluate project scope and communicate about agreed work.

Hosting and logs

The website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process routine technical request information for service operation and security under their own policies. This site does not itself install client-side analytics or track visits.

QR links

A campaign link may include an optional source label such as ?src=qr, ?src=email, ?src=referral or ?src=linkedin. If you choose to send an email using one of these links, the source is inserted into the draft message. The website does not itself store this parameter or transmit an analytics event; the hosting provider may still process ordinary URL requests.

Confidential material

Please do not email passwords, access keys, case files, financial information or sensitive end-client data in an unsolicited inquiry. Project-specific confidentiality, AI processing restrictions, access methods and retention practices must be agreed before any sensitive material is exchanged.

Contact

For a question or a correction request: webtrafficstrategic@gmail.com.
