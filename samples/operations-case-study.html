Content operations: from brief to agency handoff.

A transparent account of SEO content operations and digital marketing production work for professional-service and local-service clients, using historical assignment lists, project records and time logs.

What the underlying records demonstrate

Historical agency-side records show project coordination, keyword research, article creation on specifically logged tasks, editing and posting workflows, indexation-related work, and coordination of external writers and website contributors. These duties are a credible basis for offering production-management and SEO content operations services.

The value is not “I wrote every page.” It is “I can manage the steps between a content brief, an approved deliverable, publication readiness and handoff.”

Typical workflow

  1. Review approved keyword and service-page targets for the client's practice or market.
  2. Structure an article brief, source requirements and appropriate editorial review.
  3. Coordinate drafting, revisions and final acceptance with the assigned specialists.
  4. Review headings, internal links, metadata and content consistency before publishing.
  5. Prepare implementation notes and track follow-up verification.

What this does not claim

This is an experience case study, not a client-endorsed case study or permission to display confidential documents. Published third-party legal content is not represented as solely authored without separate authorship and reuse verification. No specific increase in traffic, conversion rate, ranking, revenue or client retention is claimed.

For regulated professional topics, published content and legal assertions require appropriate professional review and up-to-date authoritative sourcing.

Provenance: Personal work logs and campaign schedules were reviewed to establish the scope of involvement. The underlying private files are not redistributed. This page is a concise public summary of permissible general activities, not the original client material.
